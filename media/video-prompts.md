# JustMyLuck Analog Video Prompts

Use these for the six video slots currently wired into the site. Keep clips short, loopable, and mostly text-free. The app supplies labels, narrative copy, and UI text, so the videos should feel like evidence rather than exposition.

General style for every tape:
Analog horror, cursed personal website, dying laptop in an empty apartment, degraded VHS or MiniDV, CRT bloom, tracking noise, faint film grain, practical mundane objects, subtle wrongness, no gore, no cheap jump scare, no readable generated text, no obvious monsters, beautiful dread, slow camera language, loopable final frame.

## `tape-00-boot.mp4`

Homepage mood tape: "it was supposed to be a game."

Prompt:
A dying laptop open on a scratched apartment desk at night, displaying a minimal black personal website with an unreadable clover-like icon. The room is empty but feels recently occupied: coffee ring, unpaid bill, loose key, graph paper notes, a cracked phone face-down. The screen flickers with old web-page glow. Reflections in the laptop bezel lag one frame behind the room. A tiny pressed clover lies near the trackpad with one leaf missing. Subtle VHS tracking, CRT-like bloom, soft dust in the air, eerie but elegant, no readable text, loopable.

Suggested duration: 8-12 seconds.

## `tape-01-signal-break.mp4`

The Package / wrong envelope intrusion.

Prompt:
Close-up analog footage of a sealed envelope on a cheap kitchen table. No return address. The stamp is visibly wrong but unreadable. A pressed clover silhouette can be seen through the paper. Over several seconds, the envelope rotates a few degrees toward the camera without a hand touching it. The table lamp flickers. For three frames, the envelope appears already open, then sealed again. The missing clover leaf looks like a tiny keyhole. Damaged VHS, amber-green shadows, no readable text, no gore, loopable ending with the envelope exactly where it started.

Suggested duration: 10-14 seconds.

## Optional Sound Direction

- Tape hiss, low apartment hum, fluorescent buzz, distant dial tone.
- Keep voices indistinct and reversed unless you plan to edit exact lines yourself.
- Avoid loud stingers. The site is scarier when it feels patient.

## Export Notes

- Export MP4/H.264 if possible.
- 16:9 is safest for the current layout, but 4:3 inside a 16:9 frame also fits the tone.
- Keep files under roughly 20-30 MB each for local browser smoothness.
- The app starts videos muted with controls, so audio is optional.
